# PolyPeek – Privacy Policy

PolyPeek does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. The only data it keeps is stored locally in your browser and never leaves your device.

## What the extension does

- It runs only on **sketchfab.com**.
- It requests public model information (triangle count, vertex count and AI-generated status) from sketchfab.com to display it on the page.
- To reduce requests, these results are cached locally in your browser (`localStorage`) for up to 7 days.
- Your "Hide AI" preference is stored locally in your browser.

## Data sharing and analytics

No data is sent to the developer or to any third party. There is no analytics, tracking, or user account system.
